What Is Audifort?

Audifort is marketed as a dietary supplement designed to support hearing wellness. The label identifies chromium plus a 200 mg proprietary blend and recommends shaking the bottle well before use.

The suggested use on the label says to take 1 or 2 droppers full daily. Because this is a supplement, it should not be presented as a cure or guaranteed solution for tinnitus, hearing loss or another hearing condition.
