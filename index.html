What Is Audifort?

Audifort is marketed as a dietary supplement designed to support hearing wellness. Its label identifies a 200 mg proprietary blend plus chromium, and the suggested use says to shake well and take 1 or 2 droppers full daily.

This distinction matters: Audifort is not a drug and should not be presented as a cure or treatment for tinnitus, hearing loss, or another medical condition. Hearing changes deserve appropriate medical evaluation.
